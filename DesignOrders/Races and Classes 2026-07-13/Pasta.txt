	##### Cover Art #####

Hello,

I am looking for a concept artist to help me with the cover art of our tabletop roleplaying game, QuestGuard.

The cover art will be on a hardcover book.

Here are the style specifications:
- Cover art is of a team of adventurers of various fantasy races (an Elf, a Human, etc), in an action pose, looking in all directions.
- The style required is concept-art like, with thick outlines, refined sketch-like art, with a slightly desaturated but colorful color palette. It's stylized art, cartoony leaning into anime/comics/manga. The coloring must contain gradients, so only flat colors.
- The resolution must be at least 2550 × 3300 pixels (300dpi)
- The logo of the game will be at the top of the page, and the artwork goes below

Description of the artwork:
- There are 5 characters arranged from left to right as a team, overlapping at the base; together, their artwork is somewhat circular
1. A fox-folk hunter on the far left. She is wearing a green cape (Robin Hood-like), wielding a bow and has a sword on her 
2. A steampunk human female next on the left
3. A giant cyclops knight, leaning to the right (no helmet). He has a gigantic greatsword, in the ground (or maybe in another pose that looks cool). He has more pronounced face features, more rough, and square-ish.
4. A dwarf female warlock on the far right casting purple magic. She has dwarven motives on her clothing and hair. Maybe she has a stone rune on her.
5. A large purple dragon on the middle, in the back
- The artwork is surrounded by purple flames and particles forming a nice circle
- There are minor UI elements around, like maybe a border around the art or the page
- No background
- The general color palette of the game is white, black and purple, so the art should use these colors.

I would like the final artwork to be separated by several layers so we can easily make modifications if needed (e.g. each character should have multiple layers, for the line art, colors, and maybe even different body parts)

I attached 2 concept arts made by AI. The final art should be somewhat in that style, but more spaced out and less cluttered.

I also attached some other games which are an artwork over some white background.

I would love to have discussion about specifics and see if you are fit for this job!
